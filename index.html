Section 1 / 6

Client Goal & Directive

🎯  GOAL
  • Accelerate delivery of a new Sales & Renewals Pipeline application for desktop and mobile
  • Launch by October 2026 to meet a critical sales cycle start date
⚡  DIRECTIVE
  • Experiment with "pre-build first" approach to develop a fully functional sandbox POC for validation and dev baseline
  • Operate as a lean team of cross-competency experts to drive vision, architecture, and experience
  • Bypass Figma and defer agile methodology until baseline POC is established

Value & Efficiency Gains

Tangible outcomes from the pre-build agentic approach

🚀

Speed to Value

  • Faster experience & solution validation
  • Stronger stakeholder buy-in
  • Accelerated agile process
  • Fast-tracked development
  • Reduction of costly rework
  • Increased client satisfaction
⚙️

Efficiency Gains

  • Reduced manual Figma work involving component creation, prototyping, and file management (Note: Figma Make may address this pain point)
  • Reduced development time with tangible implementation baseline
  • Reduced custom front-end dev efforts with polished first drafts by UX team using Cursor
  • Reduced meeting time and features/story refinement efforts
